Hi!

Hi, I’m Vanessa. I’m a biologist interested in how biological systems work and change. My work brings together genomic, morphological, and functional perspectives, drawing on both established and emerging approaches as the questions call for them. Bringing these perspectives together can address questions that are difficult to approach through any one of them alone, with the biology of the organism helping shape the work.

Outside of science, I spend a lot of time exploring the world with our young daughter, while trying to make room for some things of my own—getting back on the water and figuring out what will grow in our shady native garden.
