Hi!

I am a computational and evolutionary biologist with a background in genomics, bioinformatics, and molecular biology. My research has spanned diverse biological systems, from comparative and evolutionary genomics across animal species to infectious-disease genomics, with a particular interest in using sequencing and computational approaches to investigate biological variation and evolution.

I currently work at the Smithsonian's National Museum of Natural History on the development of a genetic resource repository for freshwater mussel research and conservation. Previously, I conducted postdoctoral research at the NIH Vaccine Research Center, where I developed and applied sequencing and bioinformatics approaches to study viral genetic diversity and evolution.

Check out my project page to read more about some of my work, or visit the resume tab for additional information. You can also send me a message if you would like to talk about genomics, bioinformatics, science, or dogs.
